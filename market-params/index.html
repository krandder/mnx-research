MNX Proposal — pre-launch parameter audit

Launch parameters: what should change

Five changes before real launch, in priority order: add a minimum order floor (there is none), make the ANTHROPIC/OPENAI tick 10× finer, verify the live oracle jump cap, raise the per-account open-notional tiers, and decide the zero-fee wash-trading question. The take-bounds stay where they are — on the futures they are the only per-batch cap on a self-referential oracle. Everything else, including the CRWV/SPCX tick, should stay.

Tick sizes

The evidence gives a narrow target. Liquid perp venues cluster relative tick at 0.1–1.3 bps (Hyperliquid pins it to 0.1–1.0 bps by construction; Binance and dYdX majors sit inside it; QFEX quotes CRWV and SPCX at 1.13 and 0.69 bps — the same underlyings as MNX). The empirical comfort band is 2–15 ticks inside the typical spread (SEC DERA: below 2 the tick binds and spreads pin; above 15 quality degrades in continuous markets). The coarse outliers — dYdX's $1 ticks on $2,000-priced assets at 4–6 bps — are acknowledged defects being repaired by governance.

The FBA tilt: when in doubt, err fine. Budish, Cramton & Shim address tick size in frequent batch auctions directly: the message-traffic argument against fine ticks "is moot here, because orders are opaque during the batch interval," coarse ticks increase rationing at the margin, and they "conjecture that the optimal tick size in a frequent batch auction is at least as fine as in the continuous market." The classic fine-tick harms (queue-priority races, penny-jumping for time priority, quote-flicker spam) are artifacts of continuous matching that MNX's sealed batches don't have; the coarse-tick harms (clearing-price rounding noise, a binding spread floor, more pro-rata rationing ties) survive. So MNX should sit at the fine end of the band.

Why the futures tick matters mechanically

The market maker's ladder grid on ANTHROPIC/OPENAI is $2 — currently just 2 ticks. Its reanchor guard is half a grid step (= 1 tick) and bid/ask rounding moves prices by up to a tick (= half a grid step): the whole quoting apparatus sits at the quantization edge. At tick 0.1 the same grid is 20 ticks and every derived threshold gets real resolution. The MM reads tick/step from the exchange API at runtime, so the change propagates with no MM deploy.

Standing recalibration policy

Adopt a published trigger instead of ad-hoc revisits: recalibrate a market's tick when its median quoted spread is < 3 ticks (too coarse) or > 30 ticks (too fine), checked monthly. Precedent: the SEC now literally reassigns US-equity ticks semiannually on a spread ≤ 1.5-tick trigger, and dYdX's governance runs batch repairs on the same logic. setTickSize is freely settable both directions — tick is not a one-way door, just a coordinated config + DB re-materialization.

Take-bounds and the ADL invariant — keep them

The take-bounds (mtbLong/mtbShort — how far a batch clearing price may sit from the oracle) should stay where they are: ±5% on the stocks, ±20% on the futures. The decisive reason is what the oracle is on each market. CRWV and SPCX have external oracles (stock aggregator, Hyperliquid after hours) — there the band protects fills against a lagging reference, and gaps move the oracle, not the band. ANTHROPIC and OPENAI have no external reference at all: their oracle derives from MNX's own market. On a self-referential oracle the take-bound is the only per-batch cap on how fast trading can drag the oracle — and every liquidation trigger with it. A wider band there is a wider manipulation handle, not better price discovery: a single print at −22% would already exceed the futures' entire maintenance margin (16.7%). The past +24% OPENAI dislocation is an oracle problem with an oracle fix in flight (the mark-price rework), not evidence the band is too tight.

Market integrity floor

Minimum order: none exists today

The effective floor is minTradeQty × price ≈ $0.09 on CRWV. No off-chain notional gate, and the MM's own min-notional check is unwired for MNX markets. Venue practice clusters hard at $5–10 (Hyperliquid $10, Binance 5 USDT, Bybit 5 USDT, Paradex $10, dYdX ≈$1–10 by design), with spam and chain-state bloat as the stated reasons. MNX has a sharper reason than any of them: every position settles on-chain — funding keeper batches run ~5.3M gas per 25 accounts, and dust accounts join every liquidation, ADL, and settlement sweep forever. Set minTradeQty to the ≈$10-equivalent per market (change #2); step size stays 0.001.

Zero fees × trade-driven signals

Zero fees are a fine launch promotion, but three signals derive from batch prints: the funding sample is the clearing price, the mark input includes last trades, and candles are trades. With 0/0 fees, a self-crossed pair inside a batch costs nothing, carries no market risk, paints all three signals, and MNX pays the settlement gas. Two acceptable resolutions, either is fine: verify the FBA prevents self-matching (same-account buy/sell in one batch), or charge ≥1 bp taker so painting has a price. Deciding neither is the only wrong option. Fees are freely settable both directions (setDefaultMakerFee/TakerFee), so this does not block launch.

Keep list — stated, not implied

  • CRWV/SPCX tick 0.01 — at venue norm exactly; no evidence of tick constraint.
  • Step size 0.001 everywhere — step notionals $0.09–$2.6 sit inside the comparable venue range; with the $10 order floor added, step is display granularity only.
  • Leverage 10×/5×/3×/3× — CRWV matches Hyperliquid's 10×; the futures' 3× is what buys their wide take-bounds (see the coupling). Any change re-runs the invariant.
  • Funding caps 10 bps/hr on the perps — the funding design is its own decision record at /funding; nothing there needs to move this week.
  • All take-bounds as deployed (±5% stocks, ±20% futures) — on the futures the band is the only per-batch cap on a self-referential oracle; on the stocks the external oracle absorbs gaps. See the invariant section.
  • Zero maker fee — keep regardless of the taker-fee decision; maker rebates can come later.
