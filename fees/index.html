MNX Proposal — fee schedule

Fees: zero as a labeled promo, 2.5 bps taker as the published endgame

Launch at 0/0 explicitly labeled as a promotion with a date, and publish the permanent schedule on day one: 0 maker / 2.5 bps taker, flat, no tiers, no rebates. The pre-commitment converts the later fee introduction from a betrayal into a kept promise — the exact mistake Paradex made in reverse.

The decision (F1–F4)

#DecisionRecommendedWhy
F1 Structure Flat, no tiers, no rebates. Maker 0 permanently (until external makers exist, and even then a small positive fee — never a rebate). Maker rebates compensate queue risk under continuous price-time priority; MNX's batch auction has no queue by construction, so the rebate rationale is void (Budish et al.; OneChronos — the closest TradFi analogue, ~10 auctions/sec — charges flat symmetric fees, explicitly no maker-taker). And today the only maker is MNX's own MM: a rebate is a self-payment that fabricates revenue/expense on the books. Tiers are pointless with one maker and no whales — add later if real flow demands.
F2 Launch schedule 0/0 labeled "launch promotion until <date, 4–6 weeks out>"; permanent schedule published at launch: 0 maker / 2.5 bps taker. Date trigger, not volume trigger. Right to end early "for market integrity" reserved in the announcement. Growth ≫ revenue now and the zero headline is free — but Paradex promised "always-on, not a promo" and imposed a taker fee 6 months later anyway; pre-committing the endgame keeps the promise instead of breaking one. 2.5 bps flat is exactly what Hyperliquid charged when it exited its own zero-fee alpha, and undercuts every current non-promo VIP0 taker rate (HL 4.5, Velocity 3.5, dYdX 5, Binance 5, Kalshi 12) except Paradex retail (0.75) and Lighter (0). Volume-triggered sunsets are gameable; dates aren't.
F3 Wash defense during the promo Per-account fill-rate limits + volume/OI alerting (flag >5x) + the reserved early-end right. MNX's self-trade prevention is airtight for same-account fills within a batch (verified in the match engine), but it cannot price two colluding accounts, cross-batch round trips, or one-sided tape-painting — and at 0/0 all three are free while MNX pays settlement gas per fill and the prints feed our own funding/mark/candles. Lighter, with a $920M USDC float subsidizing zero fees, still hit a 27x volume/OI wash blowout on monitoring-only defense. The promo window carries this risk knowingly and briefly; the taker fee is what ends it.
F4 Documentation & change rule A public Fees page on docs.mnx.fi (generated from the same market config the engine charges, so docs can't drift), stating: decreases/promotions effective immediately; increases with ≥7 days' public notice. The promo sunset date published at launch satisfies the notice for the first increase. One venue-wide parameter-change norm (mirrors the oracle-methodology rule and the Class A/B split in /param-procedures). Kalshi's CFTC self-certification practice is the formal analogue. Today fees are documented nowhere except one soon-stale phrase in the markets guide.

Option rejected — 0/0 indefinitely (the Lighter play): Lighter sustains retail-zero with LP-vault earnings, liquidation revenue, and yield on ~$920M of USDC float — none of which MNX has — and still could only fight wash volume with points-program forensics after the fact. For MNX, wash volume is strictly negative-margin (we pay gas per fill) and pollutes inputs our own risk systems consume. Monitoring-only failed at a venue with far more resources.
