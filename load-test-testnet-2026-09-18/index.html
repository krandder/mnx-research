Forty funded load accounts placed and churned resting orders on both sides of the testnet market maker (MM) and hit it with IOC market takers for 63 minutes of generator time (22:06–23:18Z), with the additive order-hold policy from exchange PRs #1001–#1004 live. This page records what was run, what the exchange and the MM did, every deviation and its attribution, and what to re-run once the max-side hold (#1005/#1006) lands.

What was tested. Additive per-account/per-market order holds (#1001–#1004) under realistic churn: both-sides resting post_only GTT limits 3–10% away from the MM, batches of 50, cancels in batches of 100, margin-driven trimming, cancel-all sweeps, and IOC MARKET takers crossing the MM (perps/futures only from r4). The MM build under test was main 8f9de28 (ring stack: level_stride, absolute slot ring, book≤curve telemetry).
Was it green? For the load itself, yes: the exchange answered every generator request 2xx at a flat ~280 ms, the MM never failed a tick while load was running, no resting load order was ever filled, and the post-run books, balances and reservations reconcile to the cent. greenThroughout = false only because the monitor went RED 5 times: 2 episodes were an exchange deploy rolling under the test, 3 were single-poll MM-telemetry blips (dashboard 503 / gzip error / timeout). Peak throughput (33 orders/s, 589 orders in the best minute) was bounded by the toolkit's own freshness gate and per-user margin, not by the exchange.
Exchange problems found (details in Exchange problems observed): (1) testnet auto-deploy rolled the exchange twice around the test (22:16–22:20Z during r1, 23:34–23:39Z after), each costing the MM 1–4 min of failed cancels/places; (2) a read-tier outage 23:26–23:30Z (LB 503 failed_to_pick_backend ×251) with no load from this lane, during which the MM failed closed (about 20 consecutive failed ticks) and recovered on its own; (3) an on-chain/off-chain MarginBank mismatch for pool taker user 374 ($11.00 diff at the 23:00Z audit, a fill at the audit instant); (4) an uncoordinated open-loop driver from the exchange lane (34.84.233.44, up to ~330 req/s) overlapping r4/r5/r6 and immediately preceding the outage; (5) two “cancelled with sealed quantity still in flight” and two “Solvency Layer B positive residual” log lines, recorded as observations.

Toolkit limits and follow-ups

Throughput was gate-bound, not exchange-bound. Of the ~62,000 scheduled resting placements, the 6 s fresh-quote gate skipped 21,963 (MM telemetry age 4–5 s + fetch age) and per-user margin trimming under additive holds refunded 17,119 (40 users × $5,000 at 1× is ~617 resting orders of avg $275). Effective resting rate was 350–385/min against a 1,000/min schedule at full rate; peak 33 orders/s; peak 8 HTTP requests/s against a 14/s global budget. To push the exchange harder next time: relax the staleness gate (or read the runner's own snapshot), fund users higher (or wait for max-side holds, which halve the hold for two-sided books), and raise --orders / --duration-min.
  • BLOCKING before the next run — untracked resting orders after a non-2xx place batch. If the exchange processes a place batch but answers non-2xx (e.g. the LB's 503 backend_connection_closed_before_data_sent_to_client seen live at 22:16:57Z), the generator drops the batch from its tracked set: those resting orders sit on the book with no drift guard, no max-age cancel and no opposite-quote guard until the exit sweep — and post_only is inert once resting, so they are exactly the orders that could cross the MM as it moves. Reproduced on a stub (every 3rd batch stored then answered 502): 400 tracked vs 597 on the server, 197 untracked, miscounted as rejected. Fix: createSignedOrder already yields the canonical orderHash; keep {spec, orderHash} per order and on any non-ok response after retries push all of the batch's hashes into the tracked set (later stale cancels are harmless). In this run no resting order was ever filled and every sweep found 0 residual, so no harm occurred. Found by the hawk pass on the post-run commit 7b5c720 (verdict REJECT for this item).
  • Taker starvation (fixed on farol before r2, not yet on the branch head used for the run): the resting loop ran before the taker block and, because trimmed orders are refunded to the schedule, filled all 6 inflight slots on every fresh tick; r1 placed 32 takers in 11 min. With the taker block first, r6 placed 588 takers in 20 min (30/min as configured). Farol's copy = dc5978e + this patch (backup results/generator.ts.orig); it must be re-synced from the accepted head before any next run.
  • Also fixed in 7b5c720 (not used in the run): per-bucket pacing charges for cancel batches (61–100-hash cancel batches could throw on the 60-capacity global bucket and lose their victims from tracking), salt-prefix rollover from crypto rather than the seeded PRNG, provisioning balance reads charged to the global pacer, batch-size validation before pacing/network. Tests 99 / 99.
  • Monitor rules that cannot fire yet: request records carry only the final status, so 429s absorbed by requestWithRetry are invisible and the AMBER “429 rate > 5%” rule is inert (stub served 22 × 429, generator reported 0); read-side calls (fills/by-user, balance/:id/margin, orders/by-user) are not in the request sink, so per-endpoint numbers miss the read side. Any single telemetry fetch failure is RED (see MM problems).
  • Default taker set still includes ANTHTOP26 (DEFAULT_TAKER_MARKETS and the README example) although the Kelvin-approved amendment restricted takers to perps/futures; change the default.
  • Known-inert guard: post_only appears only in a deprecated block of the request schema and is read nowhere on the exchange (FBA path also ignores time_in_force); the offset / freshness / drift-guard / fill-detector stack is the only protection for the MM, hence the RED-on-any-resting-fill rule.
  • Funding cap: admin/user/fund reverts past 5,000 USDM per account (externalDepositCap); larger takers need the MarginBank owner's exemption call, which this lane did not send.
  • Exchange route quirks already handled by the toolkit (worth knowing for other drivers): admin/* and test/* routes are served at the bare path while public routes are under /v0/; client_request_id idempotency keys live 10 min and 409 on reuse with a different payload.

What to re-run once #1005 / #1006 land

Proposed by another agent and relayed by Kelvin on 2026-09-18; recorded here in substance. One real HTTP end-to-end test, to run after #1005 + #1006 are merged and deployed to testnet.
  1. Fund account A with 130; place LONG 80 and SHORT 50.
  2. Assert both are accepted, but reserved margin is 80 (the max side), not 130 (the additive policy would hold 130).
  3. Partially fill and cancel the LONG while sealed.
  4. Settle through the real HTTP / journal / materializer path.
  5. Verify terminal order state, matching live/read balances, sequence advancement, no stale claim.
  6. Cancel the remaining SHORT and verify the hold reaches zero.
scripts/run-htv1-test.sh --instance N src/tests/high-throughput-v1-http-e2e-order-holds.test.ts   # exchange repo
Decision item for the exchange team (exact-max funding guard). With exactly 80 funded, the current #1005 guard rejects the second (50) side order. The proposal includes that as a negative test. If exact-max funding must accept both sides, that guard has to change before #1006 activates max-side.

How this load test relates. Today's run stressed the additive policy live on testnet (#1001–#1004): both-sides resting orders per account/market, churn, partial fills, cancel-all, multi-market accounts. Re-running the same load after #1006 should show, for each pool user, per-market hold = max(buy, sell) + unknown rather than buy + sell. Concretely, for a resting user with both sides on a market the reserved margin should roughly halve for the same order set, so (a) the generator's margin-trimming count (17,119 in this run) should drop and the effective resting rate rise for the same $5,000 funding, and (b) the toolkit's per-user balance snapshots (total / available / reserved from /v0/balance/:id/margin, written on every minute record and in the summary) can be diffed between the two runs per user. Also re-check the two “cancelled with sealed quantity still in flight” lines, which are the live analogue of step 3.

Re-run checklist: fix the untracked-orders-after-5xx gap and re-sync farol from the accepted head; move the taker-first patch onto the branch; set takers to perps/futures by default; schedule around merges or pause testnet auto-deploy; agree a testnet load window with the exchange lane; monitor RED only on 2 consecutive telemetry failures; same pool (40 users, ids 340–379, still funded ~$198.5k) can be reused (provision is idempotent and only tops up).
