All nine markets are connectorless: no external price feed. Known maker limitation: mm-core budgets binary shorts as Q × P, but true binary short collateral is Q × (1−P) — at a 5¢ market the NO side costs ~19× its quote notional. Until fixed, low-probability markets (SSITOP, MILLPROB) will show asymmetric depth, and the TAKEOFF maker runbook requires funding the full worst-payout amount with rollback on any margin rejection. This is fail-closed (one-sided books), not a solvency risk.

Review provenance and approval state

Three adversarial rounds shaped the slate. Round 1 (#384): resolution executability — unpublishable fallback promises, manipulable terminal clause. Round 2 (#391): a NUMERIC(18,8) overflow that would strand partial deployments (caps wrote contracts first, then overflowed the market insert), LEAP resolving YES off Epoch’s backcast population, and placeholder $100M/$1B order caps. Round 3: SFHOME28 omitted from remediation, a float64 trap in the overflow guard itself (the NUMERIC max rounds to exactly 1e10, so at-most passed the first invalid value), side-aware exposure, and the MILLPROB timeline contradiction. Everything is pinned by tests: FundingCapParity (population 22), SlateLaunchProfiles (per-market economics + global NUMERIC bound), and the TAKEOFF risk-profile pin. Settlement uses the documented manual two-phase sequence (delist → register claimants immediately → parity → fund → settle); the one-shot script is not used.
Approval gates: merge lands all nine trading-disabled (safe per review). Per-market activation additionally requires: maker profile + funded capacity, operator target-fair assignment, evidence-snapshot archiving (ECI CSVs, METR results, BLS first prints, Zillow ZHVI releases, LEAP baseline), monitoring manifest entry, and the enable-window check.
