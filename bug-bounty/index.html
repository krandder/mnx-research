MNX Proposal — security

Bug bounty: ship the minimal program this week

Recommendation: publish a self-hosted security policy + interim rewards promise in 1–2 days (the Hyperliquid/Drift pattern), then convert to a Cantina Gold program in weeks 2–4. Critical payouts: 10% of funds at risk, min $25k, cap $250k — sized to MNX's TVL, not copied from GMX.

The decision

#DecisionRecommendedWhy
B1 Launch-week move Self-hosted minimal program now: /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116) + a /security page with scope, rules, safe-harbor sentence, security@mnx.fi + PGP Ships in 1–2 days with zero platform dependency. Hyperliquid — the #1 perp DEX — runs exactly this model; Drift self-hosts referencing Immunefi's severity rubric. Good-faith reporting needs a published promise, not a platform.
B2 Platform (weeks 2–4) Cantina Gold; reassess Immunefi at scale 3 free months, $250k reward limit matches MNX's size exactly, launch in days, AI+human spam triage, dYdX/Coinbase precedent. Immunefi has max researcher mindshare but 10% fees and 1–3 week onboarding; right choice later, at bigger TVL. Sherlock requires buying their audit first; HackenProof has weak DeFi mindshare (Vertex: 52 submissions, $0 paid).
B3 Payout tiers (contracts) Critical: 10% of funds at risk, min $25k, cap $250k · High $10–50k · Medium $5k · Low $1k The Immunefi "scaling bug bounty" convention (10%-of-funds, capped) at Ostium's size-appropriate numbers, not GMX's $5M cap. Worst case per bug = 0.5–2.5% of early TVL. Raise the cap as TVL grows past ~$100M.
B4 Payout tiers (API/backend/web) Critical (fund theft, unauthorized trades/withdrawals, key extraction): up to $50k · High $10k · Medium $2.5k · Low $500 MNX's TypeScript exchange backend holds real risk (the matching engine and risk checks are off-chain) — Hyperliquid and dYdX both scope their API/matching tiers; MNX must too.
B5 Rules PoC required for Critical/High · local fork only, no mainnet testing · first-reporter wins · KYC + sanctions screening before payout · USDC payouts (on a chain researchers use, not only MegaETH) · severity per Immunefi VSC v2.3 by reference Standard set across every comparable program; adopting the Immunefi rubric by reference (Drift's approach) avoids writing our own taxonomy.
B6 SLAs Acknowledge 24–72h · triage verdict ~5 business days · reward decision ≤15 days · payment ≤30 days · coordinated disclosure only after fix + authorization Cantina's contractual numbers as the anchor; dYdX-style 24h reporting requirement.
B7 Safe harbor Program-level good-faith authorization language now; adopt SEAL Whitehat Safe Harbor (on-chain registry) as a follow-up SEAL is the emerging standard for active-exploit fund recovery (Uniswap/Balancer/Pendle), vetted by Cooley and Debevoise.

What a bounty does not cover: Drift's ~$285M April-2026 incident was social engineering of multisig signers plus governance manipulation — no bounty catches that. The ops side (key ceremony, signer hygiene, pause runbook, payout approvals) is a separate checklist item and should be treated as equal in priority to the program itself.

What must exist regardless of platform

Scope package

  • Canonical table of deployed MegaETH contract addresses + repo commit in scope.
  • API scope: hostnames and endpoint families (REST + WS), explicitly including the matching engine and risk-check paths.
  • Out of scope: testnet/devnet, known issues (prior audit findings listed), compromised-admin-key scenarios, social engineering, DDoS, third-party infra, oracle latency, UI-only display bugs, attacks whose cost exceeds extractable value.

Ops package

  • Funded payout wallet (USDC) + valuation snapshot rule (price at submission time).
  • Internal triage rota + escalation runbook (who can pause contracts / freeze withdrawals).
  • Payout approval threshold (e.g. anything > $25k needs a second signer).
  • security.txt with Contact, Policy, Encryption, Expires.

Program page outline (draft, ready to adapt)

  1. Overview & philosophy
  2. Assets in scope — contracts table (address / commit / severity ceiling) + API endpoints table
  3. Impacts in scope per severity (Immunefi VSC v2.3 mapping)
  4. Rewards table + payout basis (10%-of-funds rule, min/cap, currency, valuation snapshot)
  5. Out of scope & known issues
  6. Rules of engagement (no mainnet testing, PoC, first-reporter, no extortion)
  7. Disclosure policy & SLAs
  8. Safe harbor statement (+ SEAL adoption reference)
  9. KYC / eligibility (no employees or auditors, no sanctioned persons)
  10. Contact: portal or security@mnx.fi + PGP; security.txt pointer
