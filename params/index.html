The liquidity model behind the numbers

Assumed MM equity $50,000, distributed by the current capital-scaling parametrization: book fraction 0.41, inventory fraction 0.99, capital weights ANTHROPIC=OPENAI=4, all other quoted markets 1 (weight sum 27). Per-side resting depth = 0.41 × equity × weight/27; the MM's max position = 0.99 × equity × weight/27. Max limit order = the MM's max position (a resting maker order absorbed into inventory over time). Max market order = the notional that moves the quote by the 2% slippage cap = 2 × (impact $-to-move-1%), where impact is the immediate within-1% depth, set here at 5% of the MM max position. This keeps max market order ≈10× smaller than max limit order: a taker can move us at most ~2%, while a maker can rest up to full inventory capacity. Quantities are rounded to clean unit counts.
These caps assume the current $50k parametrization and the current capital weights. If vault equity or weights move, re-derive the impact, max market order, and max limit order values from the same formulas.

Tick-size methodology

Current displayed ticks are the legacy values: one tick ≈ the market's typical 5-minute move, using median absolute 5-minute close-to-close change over 48h of Hyperliquid candles, rounded to a 1/2/5 step. Markets with no trade history (H100) or fair-price-driven markets (ANTHROPIC, OPENAI, DEEPSEEK, MOONSHOT) use modeled values from their fair-ramp rates / reference-index volatility, marked modeled below. H100's tick is unchanged (slow-published daily reference).

Bands only ratchet tighter on-chain; widening requires redeploy. Leverage. Selectable tiers are round only: {3x, 5x, 10x, 20x}. On-chain perpetual leverage caps require setMaxLeverage plus redeploy; this page is the recommendation layer, not the chain state itself.
