The liquidity model behind the numbers

Assumed MM equity $50,000, distributed by the current capital-scaling parametrization: book fraction 0.41, inventory fraction 0.99, capital weights ANTHROPIC=OPENAI=4, all other quoted markets 1 (weight sum 27). Per-side resting depth = 0.41 × equity × weight/27; the MM's max position = 0.99 × equity × weight/27. Max market order = a 3% price-impact budget (≈3× one side's depth, since the resting ladder spans well under 1%); max limit order = the MM's max position for that market. Quantities rounded to clean unit counts.
Flag for review: with the current fractions, the MM max position ($1,833 at weight 1) is smaller than the 3% market-order budget ($2,278), so limit caps sit below market caps — unusual; taken literally from the sizing rule. Second flag: caps assume the $50k parametrization — if vault equity or weights change, re-derive (scripts are one command).

Tick-size methodology

Ticks are sized so one tick ≈ the market's typical 5-minute move: median absolute 5-minute close-to-close change over 48h of Hyperliquid candles, rounded to a 1/2/5 step. Markets with no trade history (VIX, H100) or fair-price-driven markets (ANTHROPIC, OPENAI, DEEPSEEK, MOONSHOT) use modeled values from their fair-ramp rates / reference-index volatility, marked modeled below. H100's tick is unchanged (slow-published daily reference).

Notes

Funding. Hourly settlement; rate = hourly time-weighted premium / 24, clamped to the per-hour cap. All perps run the Standard 10 bps/hr cap except VIX and H100 (Very weak, 1 bp/hr — VIX deliberately, so the perp floats like a constant-maturity ~1-week future; see the funding-v2 doc). Dated futures and binaries pay no funding. Per-market half-life (divisor) ships later; until then D=24 everywhere and tiers differ by cap only. Bands. mtb is a fill bound: no fill can execute beyond oracle ±band. VIX runs ±15% (weak-tether design); everything else ±5%. Bands only ratchet tighter on-chain; widening requires redeploy. Binaries (ANTHTOP26, DPREZ, OAITOP26) are fully collateralized, no funding, no MM coverage; market orders capped at ≈$500 notional.
