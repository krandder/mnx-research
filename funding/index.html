MNX Proposal — v2 (v1 preserved here)

Funding: keep the engine, define the markets

The funding engine MNX has is correct and stays as-is. The work of v2 is a market-definition layer: every market gets three business-decided parameters — half-life, cap, session — assigned by reference class. One contract delta (the hardcoded /24 becomes per-market), and two fixes before equities carry size.

The decision

Keep the mechanism, change the definitions. The verified pipeline — batch clearing price vs oracle → time-weighted hourly premium → clamped per-second rate → lazy globalIndex accrual → settle-before-risk-check — is sound and matches industry structure. Funding stays a pure P-controller with zero rate components: no interest rate, no dividend flows, no PID. The design work is per-market:

1 · Half-life Per-market divisor How hard funding pulls perp to oracle. Today a hardcoded /24; becomes the market's strength tier.
2 · Cap Derived, not designed Max leverage is the business input; the cap fits under 0.5/maxLev and mmr/2 with orders of magnitude to spare.
3 · Session Reference-class rule Funding accrues only when the reference is live: 24/7 for crypto, RTH for equities, fresh prints for slow indices.

Two conditions before US-equity perps carry size: (1) the session-toggle path that discards unsettled accrual (FundingStatusSettlementSkipped) must settle-to-boundary or fail closed; (2) a stock-split settle-and-reopen procedure must exist — the repo currently has no corporate-action handling at all. Details in Required fixes.

Why the engine stays

Every mechanism claim was re-verified against the code (exchange @ 412c202). The premium input stays the FBA clearing price: printing a deviant uniform price means trading through the resting book at a loss, to move a signal that is time-weighted over an hour, divided by the half-life, and capped at basis points — the attack pays counterparties more than funding can extract. No-fill batches contribute nothing, which is correct: no trades, no premium evidence, no funding.

Strength = a half-life, set per market

Funding is a proportional controller: rate = premium / D per hour makes basis decay with time constant ≈ D hours. Today D = 24 is a hardcoded literal — one strength for BTC and for a daily-published compute index. That is the wrong invariant. Strength must match how much the oracle can be trusted over the next hour:

Tier Divisor D (τ) Cap /hr Assigned when
Strong 8 (~8 h) ±10 bps Deep continuous reference, 24/7 (BTC, ETH majors).
Standard 24 (~1 day) ±5 bps (±10 approved) Liquid session-bound reference (large-cap US equities in RTH, FX/metals). Current deployed configs are 10 bps at D=24.
Weak 72 (~3 days) ±2 bps Thin or noisy short-term reference (small caps, new listings).
Very weak 168 (~1 week) ±1 bp Slow-published references (daily compute indices). Premium accrues only against fresh oracle prints.

The one contract delta

Replace the literal 24 in FundingOracle.setFundingRate() with a per-market fundingDivisor set at initialize (owner-updatable, like maxFunding), plus the field in deployment configs. Everything else — window, index, settlement, keepers — is untouched.

Caps are derived from leverage, not designed

Max leverage (1/imr) is the business decision. The existing tests bound the cap: maxFundingRate × maxLev ≤ 0.5 and ≤ mmr/2. At 20× those permit up to 2.5%/hr — the bps-level caps above are chosen from manipulation economics and trader UX, then verified against the bound. Note the interaction: beyond cap × D of basis the cap binds and all tiers behave alike; a weak tier is only weak if its cap is lower too.

Market taxonomy — by reference class, not asset name

The AI-asset universe (chips, clouds, compute costs, model-company proxies, event outcomes) spans every reference quality. The market definition is the row you assign, and the row fixes all three parameters:

Prediction perpetuals: when leverage on probabilities works

No venue has shipped funded leveraged perps on probability underlyings (Polymarket's and Kalshi's 2026 "perps" are on ordinary assets; Hyperliquid made its event markets fully collateralized) — an open edge, and the failure modes are precise enough to invert into viability conditions. There are exactly two hazards: terminal jump (resolution snaps price to 0/1, so leverage above 1/Jmax — the worst plausible jump — is guaranteed bad debt on the wrong side) and boundary weakness (funding proportional to q − I vanishes near the bounds exactly where relative mispricing explodes).

Condition for leverage Test AI markets
Long-dated, low resolution hazard Known resolution date, far away; no surprise-resolution path ("will X resign" fails at any leverage). Most AI-milestone markets qualify.
Jump-bounded leverage Lmax ≤ 1/Jmax, compressed toward 1× on a schedule as resolution approaches; halt-and-cash-settle at the pre-resolution mark. Far-out Jmax ≈ 0.2–0.3 → 3–5× is honest.
Right coordinate or bounded payoff One of the two mechanisms below. —

Commit now — range-collateralized leverage

The near-term product. Leverage as narrowed bounds: a position collateralized only over [p_lo, p_hi] is 1/(p_hi − p_lo)× levered, fully collateralized inside the range, zero bad debt by construction, and needs no funding at all. This is MNX's existing numeric-future min/max machinery with tighter bounds (Levana runs the capped-payoff equivalent in production). Cost: exposure truncates outside the range. Ships leveraged probability exposure while the logit-perp is validated.

Research program — the logit-perp

Trade log-odds ln(p/(1−p)) instead of p: unbounded and roughly symmetric, so the boundary pathology is exposed as a coordinate artifact and the standard P-funding of this document (logit basis ÷ D, capped) applies unchanged. Margin and liquidation live in the same coordinate — which is exactly what the published boundary-corrected scheme didn't do (it patched funding while margining in probability space, and failed its backtests). TickSpread's engine already had a log_perpetual type. Validation gate: backtest funding + margin in log-odds space on public prediction-market tick data against pre-registered floors; build only if it clears them.

Sessions and weekends

Launch policy: frozen funding, live oracle

Outside RTH the oracle already tracks Hyperliquid xyz (which itself follows its book via a 30-min EWMA with discovery bounds on weekends), so marks, liquidations, and ADL stay honest while funding accrual is frozen. The unanchored-price cost is bounded in practice by that oracle: weekend drift shows up in margin requirements even without funding pressure. This is the current architecture; v2 keeps it and fixes only the toggle (fix 1).

Documented option: weekend weak-tether

If weekend basis proves persistent, enable accrual outside RTH at the weak tier against the existing HL-based oracle, with a per-closed-session accrual budget of ≤ 40 bps as a hard bound on imported noise. Precedent: trade.xyz itself halves funding strength during weekend discovery. Risks to accept knowingly: HL xyz is >90% of HIP-3 equity OI (every competing dex died) — a single external dependency — and its weekend oracle is its own book. This is a config flip, not new infrastructure.

Corporate actions and listing gates

Dividends: no payments, no index adjustment — industry standard, not a shortcut. QFEX: "does not adjust positions or margin for most corporate actions, including cash dividends… the market prices in these events." Hyperliquid single-stock perps have no dividend policy either. The market discounts the ex-date drop in advance and funding (longs receive while perp < index) absorbs the residual; at 5 bps/hr ≈ 84 bps/week of capacity this works for the ~0.1–0.3%/quarter dividends of AI-sector names.

Splits are not dividends — mandatory procedure

A 10:1 split is a −90% index print that liquidates every long. Adopt QFEX's settle-and-reopen: halt before the effective date, realize PnL at the last mark, reopen positions at quantity × ratio, price ÷ ratio (notional-neutral). MNX has no corporate-action code today (fix 2) — this procedure must exist before listing any split-candidate name.

Listing gates replace mechanisms

Everything that would have been a rate component becomes a listing criterion instead: dividend yield ≲ 2%/yr (special dividends → halt/flag), no heavily-shorted hard-to-borrow small caps (their structural discount fights funding), and a reference that earns at least the Weak tier. Words in the listing policy, zero parameters in the protocol.

Rejected designs — and what would reopen them

Design Why rejected Reopen if
PI / PID funding TickSpread already ran one: an ad-hoc integral (gain 1/5, sign-flip resets, quadratic dead-band decay, magic constants, Decimal precision 8) in a single-point-of-failure watcher — undocumented stability. In-house evidence, not theory. Integral action also fights persistent basis that is the price signal, is loadable by an attacker who repositions, and makes carry path-dependent for MMs. RAI's arc (P-only launch, leaky-integrator PI enabled a year later under strict anti-windup preconditions) shows the cost of doing it properly. A sub-cap basis persists for weeks, demonstrably from positioning rather than oracle bias, with measurable cost.
Impact-depth sampling (v1's core proposal) Solves a manipulation problem the FBA clearing price doesn't have at MNX's caps, at the cost of new contract surface and thin-book impact prices that are cheaper to steer with resting orders than prints are with real fills. Evidence of clearing-price funding manipulation that the cap arithmetic failed to deter, or chronically fill-less markets that need a funding signal.
"HL-implied spot" (inverting their funding out of their mark) Degenerates: their weekend premium is measured against their own drifting oracle, so the inversion recovers noise; the correction is tens of bps while weekend information is percents. HL publishes a genuine external-price series worth the machinery (their frozen "external price" field is not it).
Interest / borrow-rate components QFEX ships zero interest; dYdX runs 0 on cross markets. Complexity without a user, and HTB economics are handled by listing gates. MNX introduces non-USDC collateral with a real carry differential.

Required fixes before equities carry size

  1. Session-toggle accrual discard (confirmed in code, by design). When the after-hours job disables funding, the Guardian low-level-calls updateGlobalIndex(); if the oracle is stale that reverts, the Guardian emits FundingStatusSettlementSkipped and proceeds to zero the rate — the elapsed accrual is permanently lost (Guardian.sol:181-217; the updater's own comments acknowledge the discard). Fix: settle-to-boundary (refresh the oracle, then settle, then disable) or fail closed (refuse to toggle until the index is fresh). Never silently skip.

  2. Corporate-action procedure. Implement the split settle-and-reopen flow (halt → realize at last mark → reopen qty × ratio, price ÷ ratio) as an operational runbook plus whatever minimal contract support settlement requires. Gate any split-candidate listing on it.

  3. Per-market divisor. The fundingDivisor contract delta from the half-life section — small, isolated, and the prerequisite for the Weak/Very-weak tiers that the AI-index roadmap needs.
