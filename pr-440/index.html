Capitalization harmonization

One casing rule for every user-facing descriptive title and subtitle across the exchange and docs. Shipped as exchange PR #440 — casing only, zero rewording.

Generated 2026-08-09 from the audit→verify workflow output. Status: PR open for review.

What was inconsistent

Market subtitles mixed Title Case descriptive phrases (“Millennium Prize Problem Solved Before 2028”, “US Inflation Rate, December 2026”) with canonical instrument names that are inherently title-cased (“iShares 20+ Year Treasury Bond ETF”) — prose propositions and proper names were indistinguishable. The docs were split internally too: the sidebar and homepage are dominantly sentence case (“Markets & margin”, “Trading fees”), while the market-specs page titled itself “Market Specifications” against its own nav entry.

The convention

Sentence case for every descriptive title and subtitle — capitalize the first word only — with three standing exceptions:
  1. Proper nouns and institutions keep their capitals: Anthropic, Taiwan, Senate, San Francisco, Millennium Prize Problem, December.
  2. Tickers, acronyms, initialisms keep canonical casing: AI, US, IPO, ETF, ETN, ADR, CPI, SSI, ARC-AGI-2, H100, S&P 500.
  3. Canonical product / instrument / company names are untouched byte-for-byte.
Why sentence case: it matches the docs’ existing dominant style and standard product-copy practice (Stripe, Linear, Google style guides), it makes forecast subtitles read as the plain-English propositions they are, and it needs zero changes to canonical names — the things that looked title-cased before were mostly proper names, and they stay exactly as their owners spell them.
